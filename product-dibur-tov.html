The Exploration

Words shape worlds.

The Challenge

Words carry weight

Words shape families, friendships, workplaces, and communities — they can strengthen people or hurt them. Many genuinely want to speak more thoughtfully, but struggle with consistency and awareness.

The Opportunity

Gentle awareness

Technology can quietly increase awareness of our communication habits — and encourage more thoughtful, intentional interactions, without ever feeling like judgment.

The Vision

Intentional speech

To create tools that help people become more intentional about the impact of their words — encouraging, dignified, and woven naturally into real life.

Rooted In

A timeless value, thoughtfully supported.

Dibur Tov explores how technology can support shmiras halashon and kinder communication — building peace and unity, one word at a time.

The concept

A small, kind nudge — never a lecture.

A quiet daily moment to set an intention, notice a habit, and celebrate small wins in how we speak to one another. Encouraging, private, and easy to live with.

This is an early design exploration — the experience will evolve as we learn from the people it's for.
