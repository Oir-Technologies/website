The Problem

A quiet, complex need

Helping a family marry off a child is sensitive, costly, and complex — coordinated discreetly across many hands, often on spreadsheets and trust.

The Solution

Coordinated with dignity

A discreet platform that coordinates support, manages vendors, and tracks milestones — so nothing is dropped and no one is embarrassed.

Families & funds

Families, community funds, donors, and volunteers working together to make a simcha possible.

Privacy & Security

Privacy isn't a setting. It's the foundation.

Encryption everywhere

Profiles and messages are encrypted in transit and at rest — sensitive information stays that way.

Data minimization

We collect only what a match genuinely requires. Nothing is mined, profiled, or sold — ever.

Consent & control

Nothing is shared without explicit, revocable consent — and every access is recorded in the audit log.
