Trust, Privacy & Responsible AI

Powerful tools, used responsibly.

AI should make your organization stronger without compromising trust. These commitments guide every system we build.

Human review on every workflow

AI prepares the work; your staff make the decisions. Important output is always reviewed by a person before it goes out.

Data minimization

We only use the information a workflow genuinely needs — nothing more — and avoid collecting sensitive details unnecessarily.

Access control

People see only what they need to. Access is limited, deliberate, and reviewed.

You own your data

Your data is yours. We never sell client data, and you can take it with you.

No training on your confidential data

We do not train public AI models on your confidential information without your explicit authorization.

Clear limits & staff responsibility

We're honest about what AI can and can't do. Final decisions always rest with your staff, and we review automated workflows regularly.

We don't make legal-compliance claims we can't back up. If your organization has specific regulatory requirements, we'll discuss them honestly during your assessment and document how each workflow handles your information.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Will AI replace our staff?
No — and that's the point. Our systems are designed to remove repetitive work so your team can spend more time on people and judgment. Every workflow keeps a person in control, and staff review and approve important output.
How much does it cost?
Most organizations begin with an AI Operations Assessment, starting at $750. Workflow implementations start at $3,000, and ongoing support starts at $500 per month. Final pricing depends on the specific workflows you choose, and you'll always see the scope and price before work begins.
Do we need to be technical?
Not at all. We handle the technical work and explain everything in plain language. We also train your staff so the systems are easy to use day to day.
Is our information kept private?
Yes. We practice data minimization, keep access controlled, and you own your data. We don't sell client data, and we don't train public models on your confidential information without your authorization. See Trust, Privacy & Responsible AI for the full list of commitments.
Which tools do you work with?
We build inside the tools you already use wherever possible — your email, spreadsheets, forms, and CRM. The assessment includes a review of your current tools so we can recommend the simplest, most reliable setup.
How long does it take?
An assessment is typically completed within a couple of weeks. A first workflow is often live within a few weeks of a signed scope of work, depending on complexity.
What if we only want one small thing?
That's a great way to start. We often begin with a single workflow that solves one clear pain point, so you can see the value before expanding.
How do payments work?
Projects begin after a signed scope of work and an initial payment, so expectations are clear on both sides. We'll send a simple invoice — there's nothing to set up on your end.
