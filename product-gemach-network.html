Overview

Generosity, finally discoverable.

The Problem

Isolated gemachs

Dozens of gemachs operated in isolation. Nobody knew what was available where, so resources sat unused while neighbors went without.

The Solution

One shared map

A unified network with a live map, searchable inventory, and simple borrowing — every gemach independent, yet discoverable together.

Who It's For

Givers & neighbors

Gemach operators and the neighbors they serve — anyone with something to lend, or a need to meet.

Rooted In

A mission, not just a map.

Every shared item is an act of chesed made easier — connecting neighbors who want to give with those who need.

Privacy isn't a setting. It's the foundation.

Encryption everywhere

Profiles and messages are encrypted in transit and at rest — sensitive information stays that way.

We collect only what a match genuinely requires. Nothing is mined, profiled, or sold — ever.
