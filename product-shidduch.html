Overview

A private process, given structure.

The Problem

Run on memory

Matchmaking lived in spreadsheets, phone calls, and notebooks. Deeply personal information was shared informally, and promising matches were lost to disorganization.

The Solution

Structure with dignity

A private platform that gives shadchanim real structure and gives singles real control — considered profiles, intelligent suggestions, and a respectful introduction flow.

Who It's For

Every generation

Shadchanim, singles, and the families and organizations who support them — designed to feel familiar and safe whatever your comfort with technology.

Rooted In

A mission, not just an app.

The goal is never to replace shadchanim — it is to empower them, and to help create more opportunities for Jewish homes, with Hashem's help.

Ahavas Yisrael Chessed Supporting Jewish Families Building Jewish Homes

Community trust

How It Works

Four quiet steps.

Create a profile

A guided, private intake captures the essentials — with dignity, never a questionnaire.

Receive suggestions

Aligned matches surface, reviewed and refined by a trusted shadchan.

Request an introduction

Both sides opt in before a single detail is shared between them.

Move forward

Track progress and stay supported, at your own pace and on your own terms.

Privacy & Security

Privacy isn't a setting. It's the foundation.

Encryption everywhere

Profiles and messages are encrypted in transit and at rest — sensitive information stays that way.

Data minimization

We collect only what a match genuinely requires. Nothing is mined, profiled, or sold — ever.

Consent & control

Nothing is shared without explicit, revocable consent — and every access is recorded in the audit log.
