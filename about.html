About Oir

We build technology that creates opportunity, strengthens communities, and helps people thrive.

Oir exists for a simple reason: the communities and organizations doing the most meaningful work are too often served by the worst technology.

Spreadsheets where there should be systems. Privacy as an afterthought. Tools built for someone else, bent to fit a mission they were never designed for.

We started Oir to change that — to bring the same craft, security, and care found in the world's best software to the people who need it most.

What We Believe

Three convictions guide everything.

01
Why Privacy Matters

Privacy is dignity.

The information people share with us — who they're looking to marry, the help they quietly need, the legacy of someone they've lost — is sacred. It is not data to be mined or monetized.

That's why privacy isn't a setting in our products. It's the foundation. Encryption, data minimization, and consent are built into the platform itself, so protecting people is never optional.

02
Why Communities Matter

Software shapes how we care for each other.

When a gemach is easy to find, more people give. When matchmaking is organized, more people are introduced. When help is coordinated, fewer people fall through the cracks.

Good technology doesn't replace community — it amplifies it. We build tools that make generosity, connection, and support flow more freely.

03
Why Trust Matters

Trust is earned, then kept.

Organizations don't just adopt our software — they stake their reputation on it. A failed system isn't an inconvenience; it's a broken promise to the people they serve.

So we build like it matters, because it does. Reliable infrastructure, honest partnership, and a commitment to be there long after launch. We're partners, not vendors.
